Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 September 2026

COVR is an iPhone app for praying at places in your neighbourhood. It is built to collect as little as possible. This policy explains what COVR collects, why, and what your choices are. COVR is operated by Matt Ayers in New Zealand (“we”).

What we store

  • Your account. You sign in with Apple. We store an account ID and the email address Apple provides. If you chose “Hide My Email”, that is a private relay address. We do not ask for your name.
  • Prayer records. When you pray at a beacon, we record that you prayed, which beacon it was, and the date and time. This powers your history and totals in the app and makes the beacon glow brighter on the map.
  • Broad themes. Each prayer can be tagged with a few broad themes, like “health” or “family”. Themes appear on a beacon only after at least two other people have prayed there. Other people never see who prayed or which themes came from you.

What we don’t store

  • Your prayer text. We process what you type or say to check it and pull out broad themes, then discard it. We never save it to our database, logs or analytics, and the app does not keep drafts.
  • Your voice. Spoken prayers are transcribed on your iPhone by Apple’s on-device speech recognition. No audio is recorded, saved or sent anywhere.
  • Your precise location. COVR uses your location while the app is open to show nearby beacons. When you pray, it sends your location once to check that you are near the beacon. That location is then discarded. COVR never tracks your location in the background.

Service providers

  • Supabase hosts our database, sign-in and server functions.
  • Anthropic (Claude) receives your prayer text to screen it for abuse and pull out broad themes. We identify you to Anthropic only by a one-way hash of your account ID. Anthropic does not train its models on this content. Under Anthropic’s standard terms, it may keep submitted content in abuse-monitoring logs for up to 30 days.
  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple, Apple Maps and on-device speech recognition.

We do not sell your data, show ads, or use third-party analytics or tracking.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in the app under Profile → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, prayer records, themes and credits, and revokes COVR’s access to your Apple ID. Beacon brightness and community themes are aggregates, so they may still reflect past activity without being linked to you.

Children

COVR is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your rights

You can ask to access or correct the information we hold about you, including under the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020. Email us at matt@hunch.tech.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. If the change is significant, we will also tell you in the app.
