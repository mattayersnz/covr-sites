COVR

Pray for your neighbourhood, where it happens.

Now in beta on TestFlight

Beacons on the map

Beacons mark real places in your neighbourhood, like a school, a park or a main street. Each one glows brighter as people pray there.

Pray on site

Walk to a beacon and pray there, by typing or speaking. Speech is transcribed on your iPhone and no recording is ever made.

Shared themes, not words

COVR never shows or stores what you prayed. Once enough people have prayed at a beacon, it shows the broad themes they share, like “health” or “families”.

Private by design

You sign in with Apple. Your prayer text and exact location are never stored, and you can delete your account from inside the app at any time.
